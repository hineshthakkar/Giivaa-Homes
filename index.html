Downtown Dubai

The freehold property development includes high-rise apartments, serviced residences, luxury penthouses, and low-rise Arab-style residences in the Old Town area. With high global demand and direct access to the Dubai Metro, not to mention proximity to the DIFC and Business Bay, Downtown Dubai continues to be a preferred destination for both end-users and investors.

Palm Jumeirah

Palm Jumeirah is one of the most renowned artificial islands in Dubai that promises an incomparable life by the sea. Palm Jumeirah is divided into three parts: Trunk, 16 Fronds, and the Crescent. The most prestigious residential area in Dubai has luxurious beach villas, high-rise apartments, and branded residences. Palm Jumeirah provides direct access to five-star hotels and restaurants along the beach.

Dubai Marina

Dubai Marina is among the most sought after localities in Dubai due to the presence of high rise buildings, active lifestyle and marina views. The locality is characterized by the development of high rises along a 3km canal which has been developed artificially. The locality has famous places like Dubai Marina Walk and Dubai Marina Mall. Besides, all the facilities needed by the locals are available nearby such as groceries, health centers and retail shops.

Emirates Hills

Emirates Hills is an exclusive gated community that is part of Emaar Properties. It is one of the projects of Emirates Living Projects located near The Meadows, The Springs, and The Lakes. This project has been inspired by Beverly Hills of the USA, and this is the epitome of luxury living. One of the unique features about this community is that it is one of the first freehold property developments in Dubai, which enables expatriates to own a house in a premium neighborhood in the UAE.

Most of the houses in Emirates Hills are built around Montgomerie Golf Club overlooking the 18-hole golf course. It is one of the most costly villa communities in Dubai with a price range of between AED 6M-AED 190M.

Jumeirah Village Circle

Jumeirah Village Circle (JVC) is an already established, family-oriented neighborhood in Dubai, owned by Nakheel. Located ideally between Al Khail Road (E44) and Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road (E311), JVC is conveniently linked to major areas such as Dubai Marina, Downtown Dubai and Mall of the Emirates.

JVC has an assortment of properties that include apartments, town houses and villas suitable for different people based on their tastes. It has more than 30 parks and many amenities including schools, nursery and shops that provide a balance in terms of residential comfort. JVC is not only affordable but also rich in facilities and developing. JVC is a favored destination for purchasing mid-range apartments in Dubai.

*One analysis found a 7.23% gross 12-month resale yield in JVC, with the largest resale transaction base among the communities it examined. Other sources report around 7–10%. Actual yields vary and are not guaranteed.
